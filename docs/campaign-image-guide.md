# Campaign Image Guide

Rules for marketing, email, and social imagery. separate from catalogue product photography.

## AI usage

**AI tools may be used only for backgrounds and scene composition**. never to alter the product itself.

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---------|-------------|
| Generated room, outdoor, or seasonal backgrounds | AI-modified product shape, color, or labeling |
| Lifestyle staging around unchanged product cutouts | AI "upgraded" or idealized product versions |
| Typography and graphic overlays on approved layouts | Products not in the verified catalogue |

## Exact catalogue products only

Campaigns must feature products that exist in `data/products.ts` with verified SKUs. Do not advertise items you do not sell or have not verified for compliance.

## Workflow

1. Start from **authorized product photography** ([image-sourcing.md](./image-sourcing.md))
2. Place on an AI-generated or licensed background if needed
3. Owner review. confirm product is unaltered and warnings/labels are legible if visible
4. Archive source files and approval date

## Sunny Bunny branding

Campaign creative uses **Sunny Bunny Toy Co.** original branding. Do not mimic the *Sunny Bunnies* entertainment property characters, color schemes, or trade dress. See [launch-checklist.md](./launch-checklist.md) for trademark review.

## Incomplete products

Do not run paid campaigns for products still marked `incomplete` or using placeholder images.
